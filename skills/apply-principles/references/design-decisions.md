# Design Decisions

## Experience first

Judge choices from the seat of the user, caller, or maintainer. Protect the central workflow and prefer a smaller polished experience without dropping requested behavior or adding scope.

## Compare meaningful alternatives

Compare alternatives when a consequential choice remains unresolved and constraints or established precedent do not settle it. Use a simple sketch or experiment only when needed to expose the tradeoff.

## Redesign from first principles

When the current shape demonstrably fights a requirement, ask what would have been built had that requirement existed from the start. Examine the affected design as a whole and choose a target around its concepts and invariants. Carry the accepted change through types, callers, and documentation in verifiable units. Do not force redesign when the existing shape serves the requirement.
